Orbital Mechanics Calculator

Project Overview

The Orbital Mechanics Calculator began as a class project and grew into a larger MATLAB tool for trajectory analysis. It studies transfer geometry, phase angles, excess velocity, and planetary flyby conditions.

I developed the calculator to better understand Earth escape and gravity assist trajectories, especially for Jupiter flyby cases.
