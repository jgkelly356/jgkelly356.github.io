VFS DBVF Hexacopter

Project Overview

The hexacopter is a competition aircraft developed for the Vertical Flight Society's Design-Build-Vertical-Flight challenge. The vehicle is intended to complete an autonomous wildfire fighting mission, requiring coordination between controls, avionics, power, structures, and payload systems.

I serve as Stability & Controls Lead, helping define the control architecture, support UAV selection, and use ArduPilot with SITL and HITL testing to validate vehicle behavior.
